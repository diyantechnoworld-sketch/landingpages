Slide 02 • Market Problem & Solution

Eliminating Distribution Inefficiencies

How B2BConnect transforms legacy FMCG & wholesale distribution bottlenecks into automated competitive advantages.

❌ Legacy Fragmented SFA + DMS

  • Ghost / Fake Orders: Salesmen fabricate orders to meet monthly targets, leading to distributor rejection.
  • Blind Spot Inventory: Brands have no visibility into secondary stockouts at regional distributors.
  • Rural Dead Zones: Web apps crash in basement Kiranas with zero internet connectivity.
  • Subjective Merchandising: Merchandising compliance relies on manual salesman check-boxes.
  • Paper Collection Leaks: Manual cash/cheque tracking results in delayed reconciliation.

✅ B2BConnect 2026 Modern Architecture

  • Retailer Verification Protocol: Asynchronous retailer OTP/push approval before distributor packing.
  • Real-time Stock Ledger: Live inventory deductions, safety thresholds, and delta audit logs.
  • True Offline-First Capture: IndexedDB local persistence with automatic background batch sync.
  • AI Computer Vision: Instant shelf photo audit with Share-of-Shelf (SoS) % & OOS detection.
  • Live Razorpay/UPI In-App: Cryptographic HMAC-SHA256 signature verification & auto-ledger sync.

Slide 05 • 2026 Innovation Spotlight

Engineered for Global Enterprise Scale

Four breakthrough architectural pillars built directly into B2BConnect.

📸 AI Vision Merchandising Engine AI Vision

Sales reps take a single shelf photo; the Computer Vision engine automatically detects SKUs with coordinate bounding boxes, computes brand Share-of-Shelf (SoS) %, and flags immediate Out-of-Stock replenishment opportunities.

⚡ True Offline-First IndexedDB Engine Resilient

Full offline execution for field sales reps. Master catalogs and routes cached in client IndexedDB. Orders, visits, and payments are queued locally and automatically synced via atomic batch API upon network reconnection.

💳 Live Razorpay & Cryptographic HMAC Fintech

Full in-app payment collection supporting UPI, NetBanking, and Cards. Backend HMAC-SHA256 signature verification prevents tampering and auto-reconciles transactions directly into the distributor credit ledger.

🔌 Turnkey Tally Prime & Busy Sync ERP Ready

Zero-middleware accounting integration. Generates Tally Prime XML Sales Vouchers and Busy Accounting JSON payloads for one-click ingestion into distributor legacy accounting software.
