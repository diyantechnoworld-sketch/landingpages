Clinical Rigor

Progress you can see in the numbers.

We don't rely on "how does it feel today?" — every session is measured against standardized clinical tools, so you and your therapist both know exactly where recovery stands.

Goniometry (ROM)

Joint angle measured in degrees at every visit to track mobility gains.

VAS Pain Scale

Standardized 0–10 pain scoring, charted session over session.

Functional Outcome Scores

Condition-specific scales (e.g. Oswestry, DASH) to track real-world function.

Strength Grading (MMT)

Manual muscle testing to objectively track strength recovery.
